# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

> "With great power comes great responsibility." - Uncle Ben

🕷️ Synopsis
Spider-Man (2002) directed by Sam Raimi is a superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. The story follows Peter Parker (Tobey Maguire), a shy high school student who gains incredible spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider. He must learn to use his new powers to protect New York City from the Green Goblin while navigating the challenges of teenage life, love, and responsibility.

📊 Why It's My Favorite
Relatable Hero Peter Parker is just an ordinary kid dealing with school, crushes, and money problems - making him one of the most relatable superheroes. |
Iconic Action The web-swinging through New York City is breathtaking and revolutionized superhero cinematography. |
Emotional Depth The relationship between Peter and Uncle Ben gives the film a strong moral core and emotional weight. |
Perfect Casting Tobey Maguire embodies both the awkward Peter Parker and the witty Spider-Man perfectly. |

🎭 Key Characters
- Peter Parker/Spider-Man - The nerdy teenager who becomes a superhero
- Mary Jane Watson - Peter's longtime crush and neighbor
- Norman Osborn/Green Goblin - The brilliant scientist who becomes Spider-Man's first major villain
- Uncle Ben - Peter's moral compass whose wisdom guides his journey
- Harry Osborn - Peter's best friend and Norman Osborn's son

Iconic Scenes
1. The Spider Bite - The fateful laboratory incident that changes Peter's life forever
2. First Web-Swinging - When Peter discovers the thrill of swinging between skyscrapers
3. The Upside-Down Kiss - The romantic rain scene with Mary Jane that became cinematic history
4. Final Battle - The emotional confrontation with Green Goblin that tests Peter's values
5. Uncle Ben's Wisdom - "With great power comes great responsibility" - the moral foundation

Memorable Quotes
> "I have a father. His name was Ben Parker."

> "Who am I? I'm Spider-Man."

> "Whatever life holds in store for me, I will never forget these words."

Final Thoughts
This movie is special because:
- It perfectly captures the essence of what makes Spider-Man such a beloved character
- It balances spectacular superhero action with genuine human drama
- The origin story remains one of the most faithful and well-told in comic book history
- It started the modern era of superhero films and still holds up today

This is my gift, my curse. Who am I? I'm Spider-Man."
